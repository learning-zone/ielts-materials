# IELTS General Training Listening

## Overview

The IELTS Listening test is 30 minutes long (+ 10 minutes transfer time in paper-based IELTS).  
It consists of **4 sections** with **40 questions** total.

| Section | Context | Speakers | Focus |
|---------|---------|----------|-------|
| Section 1 | Everyday / Social | 2 people (conversation) | Practical information (names, dates, addresses) |
| Section 2 | Everyday / Social | 1 person (monologue) | Descriptions, instructions, announcements |
| Section 3 | Academic / Training | 2–4 people (discussion) | Opinions, plans, academic tasks |
| Section 4 | Academic / Training | 1 person (lecture) | Complex ideas, academic content |

## Common IELTS Listening Tips

1. **Read questions before listening** — you have 30–45 seconds to preview each section.
2. **Follow the order** — answers usually appear in the same order as the questions.
3. **Watch for distractors** — speakers often mention something and then correct or change it.
4. **Spelling counts** — incorrect spelling means a wrong answer.
5. **Word limit is strict** — "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS" means exactly that.
6. **Listen for signpost language** — words like *firstly*, *however*, *moving on* signal new information.
7. **Plurals matter** — "book" vs "books" can change the meaning.
8. **Numbers and dates** — write digits not words unless instructed (e.g., 14 not fourteen).
9. **Transfer time** — use the 10 minutes (paper test) carefully to check answers.
10. **Practice with native-speed audio** — use BBC, podcasts, and official Cambridge materials.
