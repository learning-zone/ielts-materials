# IELTS General Training Reading

## Overview

The IELTS Academic Reading test is **60 minutes** long with **40 questions** across **3 passages**.  
Passages increase in difficulty. Texts are taken from books, journals, magazines, and newspapers.

| Passage | Length (approx.) | Difficulty | Topic type |
|---------|-----------------|------------|------------|
| Passage 1 | 700–900 words | Easier | Factual/descriptive |
| Passage 2 | 800–950 words | Medium | Analytical/discursive |
| Passage 3 | 850–1000 words | Harder | Abstract/argumentative |

**Common question types:**  
Multiple choice · True/False/Not Given · Yes/No/Not Given · Matching headings · Matching information · Summary/note/table/flow-chart completion · Sentence completion · Short answer

## Common IELTS Reading Tips

1. **Skim first** — read the passage quickly (2–3 minutes) to understand the main idea of each paragraph.
2. **Read questions before scanning** — know what you are looking for before diving into the text.
3. **True/False/Not Given** — "Not Given" means the text neither confirms nor contradicts; do not use outside knowledge.
4. **Yes/No/Not Given** — applies to opinions and views, not facts.
5. **Matching headings** — focus on the main idea of each paragraph, not individual details.
6. **Word limit** — never exceed the stated word limit; contractions count as two words.
7. **Paraphrasing** — answers in the passage are almost always paraphrased in the questions.
8. **Don\'t get stuck** — if a question takes too long, move on and return later.
9. **Proper nouns and numbers** — scan for names, dates, and statistics to locate answers quickly.
10. **Transfer time** — unlike Listening, there is no extra transfer time; write answers as you go.
